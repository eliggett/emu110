First hardware capture of the U-110's chorus and tremolo.  No factory patch enables either
effect, so nothing before this recording contained them; every trial here writes the OUTPUT
MODE explicitly to 21 (wet) or 22 (dry) along with all four effect parameters.

It settled three things: the LFO is a symmetric triangle (not the sawtooth the voice ramp law
predicted), the tremolo is an auto-pan whose sum is unmodulated, and the chorus delay tap is
the LFO level shifted right by 14, giving 1 to 32 ms.  See analysis/EFFECTS.md section 8.
